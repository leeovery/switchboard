# A doctor verb

A command that reads a period of switchboard's own records, the request ledger, the router's events,
the readings history and the logs, and reports what looks wrong in one place, with `--json` for an
agent to read.

Switchboard records almost everything a bug would leave a trace in, but nothing judges it. The logs
catch failures: a probe that errs, an upstream error, a refused token, a restart. The bugs milestone
7's reviews found leave no log line: a figure drawn wrong, or a request sent where the readings at
that moment say it shouldn't have gone. Those show only by setting one record against another, as
the ledger's request against the readings and events of its moment.

The owner chose to build it after milestone 7's release (14 October 2026).

Suggested, to settle with the owner, what it looks for:

- error and 429 rates, by account and by day;
- requests sent to an account at its limit or cap, as the readings of their moment have it;
- moves with no reason told, and holds with no event;
- probe failures, and accounts going unread;
- models the price table doesn't know, and windows nothing names;
- log warnings and errors, and the router's restarts.

It would also give the scheduled review (`2026-10-14--scheduled-review.md`) its first step.

## The owner, 26 October 2026

The owner wants more than a report: Claude able to query switchboard's data, look into its usage for problems and bugs in switchboard, and report them to the repository's GitHub issues itself, which takes in the scheduled review (`2026-10-14--scheduled-review.md`). To discuss after OAuth and the setup process. To settle: whose installs report, nothing personal in a public issue, an issue already open found before another is opened, and when it runs.

## A stale LaunchAgent

launchd reads the plist only as it loads the service, so an install keeps what the plist said when
it was written through upgrades and restarts in place. A key a newer switchboard writes, as
`ProcessType` was in October 2026, reaches an install only through `service install`, which
restarts the router; `setup` reinstalls only for another binary. Doctor could set the plist against
the one `install` would write, its options as they were given, and say to reinstall where they
differ. `setup` rewriting it itself would drop a `--log-level` given at install.
