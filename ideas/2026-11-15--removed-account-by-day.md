# A removed account's days on the dashboard

An account taken out of the config keeps its history, marked removed, under the label it had. Until
the dashboard by entity, History's Days drew it a day at a time beside the accounts still
configured. History went as a tab (November 2026), and its per-account parts moved to Accounts.
Accounts draws the configured accounts alone, so on the dashboard a removed account now shows:
- in every total, such as Compare's 12 months and Trends' YEAR;
- by week on Accounts' Tokens, by its label in faint ink, up to the week it was removed.

Its days show in `switchboard history` alone. The owner chose to accept that for now (15 November
2026), with this kept in case it's missed.

## What it could do

- **Accounts' Tokens by day:** a period of a day a row, so a removed account's days read where its
  weeks already do.
- **A removed account on Detail:** chosen as the configured ones are, its weeks and days drawn
  faint, marked removed, with no windows, forecast or sessions running.

Each wants a design pass, and Paper, before it's built.
