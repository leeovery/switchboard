# History's Days in strips per model version

History's Days draws a strip for each model family, Opus, Sonnet, Fable and Haiku, each in its
family's hue, so a family's versions share a strip. Tables name versions, Opus 5.5 and Opus 5
apart, in their shades; the charts keep to families, as four colours read at a glance and a
version's shade is hard to tell from its neighbour's.

Days could take `v`, as Year, Weeks and Tokens do, to split each family's strip into a strip for
each of its versions, should a family's versions be wanted apart, as when a new version takes over
from the one before.
