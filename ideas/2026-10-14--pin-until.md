# A pin that ends on its own

A pin that lifts itself, at a time or when its account's window resets, and routing goes back to
normal: `switchboard pin <account> --move --until 08:00`, or `--until reset`.

It's for quota that's lost if it isn't used: a fresh account, or a week about to reset with share
left. Today that takes `switchboard pin <account> --move` and then remembering to run `switchboard
pin auto` once the moment has passed; a pin left behind keeps sending every session to an account
whose reason for the pin is gone.

Raised by the owner on 13 October 2026, for a fresh account whose week reset the next morning.

Or at a level: `switchboard pin <account> --move --until 98%`, the pin lifting once its account's
use reaches it, and routing going back to auto. For an account whose window resets soon with share
left, used past its cap on purpose (the share is lost if it isn't used) but stopping short of the
last of it, kept for the Claude app. The owner raised it on 19 October 2026 with a pinned account a
few hours from its reset, weighed a notice at a level, and a pin that respects the cap rather
than passing it, and chose this.

To settle with the owner: whether `reset` means the account's 5-hour window or its week, or names
one; what the pin's line in `status` and the dashboard says of when it ends; whether a pin that
ends on its own is told as an event when it lifts; and which window a level reads (the 5-hour or
the week, or the one nearer its limit).
