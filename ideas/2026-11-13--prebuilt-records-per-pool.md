# The ledger's prebuilt records, per pool

A dashboard shows one pool (see Pools, The dashboard in a pool), the one `--pool` names, `P`
turning to the next. It filters what the router's document gives, its reads of the router's
events, read afresh at each `P`, and the accounts it lists, so Runway's LATELY, the Log's Events
and the accounts on Accounts, History and Graphs are the pool shown's alone. The Log is the pool
shown's alone too: each day's rows hold their line's pool and each chunk's meta the pools its rows
hold, so a pool's read restores only the chunks that may hold its rows, and a day's side holds the
pools of each session's lines, for its find (see The Log's In a pool). Its other reads of the
request ledger aren't filtered, as what they read is built ahead of time with no pool in it:

- **The day digests,** which a session's state rests on, for the Sessions tab and a session's page,
  and which History's and Graphs' points are read from.

So in a config with two pools, the Sessions tab, a session's page, History, Graphs and the worth
and tokens on Accounts tell of every pool's work together, labelled by account, whichever pool the
title row names; History's Year sets every pool's worth beside the plans of the pool shown.

## What it could do

- **A day's digests** hold their sessions, worth and points per pool, or a read of one pool falls
  back to the day's lines where they don't.
- **The dashboard's ledger** is read through the pool's read, as `views.PoolRead` reads the verbs'
  files, the dashboard's events and the Log's rows, so every tab tells of the pool shown alone, and
  `P`, starting the view afresh, reads the next pool's.
- **A pool the config no longer has.** The verbs that read the files read a removed pool's work,
  or another Mac's from an import, by its id (`--pool`, as `filedPool`'s kept pool reads it),
  never folded into another pool. The dashboard's `--pool` refuses one, naming the pools
  configured: it could take such a pool for History, the Log's requests and Sessions alone, its
  windows and Runway having no accounts to show, once the digests name each line's pool too, as
  the Log's rows do.

## Why it's the second phase's

Reading raw lines instead, wherever the read is one pool's of several, would be right now, but it
gives up the fast opens for exactly the user with two pools. The second phase gave the dashboard its
`--pool` and `P` first, then the Log its pool's rows; this is where its other views each come to be
one pool's.
