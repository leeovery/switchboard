# A model's window stacks USAGE on a mid-width screen

Found on 21 October 2026, reviewing USAGE's arrangement in the fluid layout pass, and kept as the
design has it.

## What happens

The Overview's USAGE sets an account's windows side by side wherever every part of its row fits
at its least: from 86 columns with two windows and the sample's names. A model's own window, as
a Fable week, is listed while it nears its limit, and a window listed is a window shown. With it,
a row holds three windows, and three need about 122 columns side by side. So from 86 to 121
columns, USAGE stacks its windows whenever any account's model window nears its limit, and goes
back to side by side once it doesn't.

## Why it matters

A tablet in landscape sits in that band, about 108 columns. There the Overview changes its
arrangement as a model's week fills up and resets, though nothing about the screen changed. It
follows the design's rules, but it's the one place a live view's arrangement turns on what an
account is doing rather than on what's configured.

## Ways it could go

- **A model's window on a line of its own** under its account's row where three don't fit, the
  week and 5-hour staying side by side.
- **Count the model window in the arrangement whenever it's listed in the config**, not only
  while it nears its limit, so the arrangement holds still.
- Leave it, since it's rare and says something true.
