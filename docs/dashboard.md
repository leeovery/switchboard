# Dashboard

The dashboard keeps your accounts' usage on screen, live, a pool's at a time where there are
several (see [Running it](dashboard/running-it.md)). It has seven tabs and a page for each session,
and shows the router's sessions and its record of every request. To open it, run
`switchboard dashboard`. Its flags are on the command's page,
[`switchboard dashboard`](commands/dashboard.md).

## The tabs

- [Overview](dashboard/overview.md): every account's windows, and how the router stands with each.
- [Accounts](dashboard/accounts.md): each account in depth, and every account together, over the
  period.
- [Projects](dashboard/projects.md): where the use went, project by project.
- [Sessions](dashboard/sessions.md): where each session's requests are going now, and every
  session of the period.
- [Runway](dashboard/runway.md): when each account has room, over the week.
- [Trends](dashboard/trends.md): the shapes of use over time, as charts.
- [The Log](dashboard/log.md): the router's requests and events, as they happen.
- [A session's page](dashboard/session-page.md): one session, through its whole life.

## Across the tabs

- [Every page](dashboard/every-page.md): the tabs, the status line, help, the key line, clicks,

- [The routing card](dashboard/routing-card.md): where new sessions go, set from the dashboard.
- [On a phone](dashboard/phone.md): how each tab lays itself out on a narrow screen.

- [Themes](dashboard/themes.md): the built-in themes, the picker, and your own.
